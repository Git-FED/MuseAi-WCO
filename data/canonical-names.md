# Canonical-name rule

The canonical name is the stable research anchor. Domains and addresses are treated as volatile observations, not as public destinations. Every rename or URL-end change should be appended with a date, prior name, new name, source, and confidence.

Known examples preserved from the brief: 9anime → AniWave; FMovies / 7movie claim marked unverified; Soap2Day mirror scale claim marked overstated.
